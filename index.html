About

Who is Yashwanth?

StudentMind started because I was tired of losing the first five minutes of every study session to prompt-crafting — so I stopped and built the fix instead of living with it. That's the pattern: notice the friction everyone just works around, then remove it.

I'm a 3rd year B.Tech AI & DS student at Parul University, simultaneously completing a Diploma in Neural Network & Deep Learning — not because the curriculum asked, but because the field moves fast and I wanted to move faster.

I lean on every tool available — AI included — the same way I'd lean on a library or a teammate: to move faster on execution while I stay accountable for the approach. I debate the design until it holds, verify what comes out the other end, and ship things complete. The measure was never which tool did the typing — it was always whether it reaches someone and helps them.

Outside of building — music, anime, and books. Currently reading Ikigai and The Art of Being Alone. Both changed how I think about what I'm doing and why.

StudentMind

Students waste the first five minutes of every AI session crafting the right prompt. StudentMind removes that barrier entirely — six LLM providers (Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, Cohere), five study modes, PDF/DOCX upload, voice input, 30+ saved sessions, one-click PDF/DOCX/Markdown export, and 16-language support. No backend. Your API key stays local.

Live ↗ GitHub

NotebookLM Prompt Vault

A multi-page, batch-aware prompt library for Parul University students built with vanilla JS ES modules. Organises 2,700+ lines of curriculum data across 7+ programs and 30+ subjects using a barrel-import architecture — cross-tab state sync, persistent theming, dual-hosted on Vercel + Netlify. Because knowing exactly what to ask is the real skill.
